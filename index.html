How to book your fitting in 3 easy steps

Message us, pick your date and occasion, and try on your favorites. Our team helps you find the right fit so you walk into your event confident and comfortable.

Slay by Ann & Rose | Dress Rental Studio

Find your dream dress and make sure it’s the perfect fit! Follow these simple steps to book your fitting appointment with us.

Step 1: Browse Our Catalogue

Explore our dress collection on our website or Facebook page. Choose the dresses you love and take note of their names or screenshots.

Step 2: Send Us a Message

Message our Facebook page, Slay by Ann & Rose Collection. Send us your chosen dress names or screenshots and tell us your preferred fitting date and time.

Step 3: Share Your Measurements

Provide your bust and waist measurements so we can help you identify dresses that may fit you best.

Step 4: Wait for Confirmation

We will check our fitting schedule and the availability of your selected dresses. Please wait for our confirmation before visiting the studio.

Step 5: Visit and Slay!

Arrive at your confirmed appointment time and try on your selected dresses. Find the one you love and get ready to slay your special occasion!

Important Reminders

  • Fitting fee: ₱100 to try up to 5 dresses (sanitizing fee). Less than 3 is free.
  • Please arrive on time for your scheduled appointment.
  • Fitting appointments are subject to confirmation and dress availability.
  • If you already have an event date, let us know so we can check the dress availability for your occasion.
